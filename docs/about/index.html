About us

About Cash4Tech

An independent, locally operated electronics buyer and reseller in Gladstone, Missouri, serving the Kansas City area.

Local and independent

Cash4Tech buys used consumer electronics directly from people in Gladstone, the Northland and across the Kansas City metro. Our store gives you a professional, comfortable place to sell your tech in person, without the hassle of selling online.

The devices we buy are tested, graded and carefully inventoried, then resold to new owners through online marketplaces. That keeps good electronics in use and out of drawers.
