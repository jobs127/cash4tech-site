FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about selling your electronics at Cash4Tech. Don't see your question? Give us a call.

What electronics does Cash4Tech buy?

We buy a range of used consumer electronics, including smartphones (iPhone and Android), tablets and iPads, laptops, desktop computers, gaming consoles and accessories, smartwatches, and computer components. We can't accept every device. Eligibility depends on the model, condition and demand. See what we buy or call to ask about your item.

How are offers determined?

Each device is evaluated in person. Offers are based on the model, specifications, physical condition, how well it works, its age and current resale demand. Prices aren't set in advance.

Can I decline an offer?

Yes. You're always free to accept or decline. If you decline, you take your device home.

What should I do before selling my phone?

Back up your data, sign out of your accounts (turn off Find My iPhone or remove your Google account), know your passcode, and remove your SIM card. Each device page has specific tips.

How should I prepare my electronics for sale?

Back up anything you want to keep, sign out of accounts tied to the device, and bring chargers, controllers or accessories you'd like us to consider.
