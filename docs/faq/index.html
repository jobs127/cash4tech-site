Selling to Cash4Tech

What electronics does Cash4Tech buy?

Phones (iPhone and Android), tablets and iPads, laptops, desktop computers, gaming consoles and accessories, smartwatches and computer components. We can't accept every device. It depends on the model, condition and demand. See what we buy or call to ask about yours.

How will I be paid?

In cash, at the store, once you accept an offer.

How are offers determined?

We evaluate each device in person, looking at the model, specs, physical condition, how well it works, its age and current resale demand. Prices aren't set in advance.

Can I decline an offer?

Yes. You're always free to decline and take your device home.

Can I get a quote online?

Not at this time. Offers are made in person at the store after we've looked at the device.

Can I mail my device in?

No. We only buy devices in person at our Gladstone store.

What should I do before selling my phone?

Back up your data, sign out of your accounts (turn off Find My iPhone, or remove your Google account), know your passcode, and remove your SIM card. Each device page has specific tips.

Buying from Cash4Tech

Are the photos of the actual device?

Yes. Every listing is one specific device, and the photos are of that device, not stock images.

Why are there several listings for the same model?

Used devices differ: storage, color, battery health and cosmetic condition. Each physical device gets its own listing so you know exactly what you're getting.

What's your return policy?

You can return an item within 30 days. The buyer pays return shipping. There's no separate product warranty.
