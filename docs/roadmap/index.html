Project roadmap · for Cash4Tech

Where this site is headed

A one-page view of the plan from Timothy's brief: what this demo covers today, and how the site grows into online sales across every channel.

In this demo Planned Needs a decision

Phase 1 · Customer acquisition website In this demo

Get local sellers through the door. This demo includes the home page, What We Buy plus six device pages, How It Works, About, Visit & Contact, and FAQ. It also has click-to-call and directions buttons on every page, a sticky call/directions bar on phones, LocalBusiness and FAQ structured data, search-friendly URLs and page titles, and tracking hooks ready for Google Tag Manager.

To launch: confirm address, hours and policies (every ⚑ on the site), add store photos, connect the contact form, set up Google Analytics 4 / Tag Manager, point getcash4tech.com here, and submit to Google Search Console and Google Business Profile.

Phase 2 · Marketing optimization Planned

Ad landing pages for searches like "sell iPhone Kansas City" and "sell phone Gladstone MO", Google Ads conversion tracking for calls and directions, remarketing with a consent notice, real Google reviews on the site, and improvements based on actual visitor behavior.

Phase 3 · E-commerce integration Planned Store set up · not launched

The Shop section already has a place in the navigation and a layout preview. When it goes live, Shopify is the inventory source of truth: every device bought in store becomes a one-of-a-kind Shopify product with its own photos, condition and test results, and Shopify handles checkout and payments. The site's Shop buttons already link to the Shopify store.

Phase 4 · Multichannel optimization Planned

One inventory, many storefronts. A device listed on eBay, the website and other marketplaces sells once, and every other listing comes down automatically. This phase adds reservations, retries, reconciliation and alerts for failed syncs.

How the pieces fit

Shopify is the single source of truth for inventory. Each device is listed once there and pushed out to every channel, so when it sells anywhere, it comes down everywhere else.
