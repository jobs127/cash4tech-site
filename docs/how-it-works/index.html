What happens in the store

When you arrive, a team member takes a look at your device. We consider its specs, physical condition, how well it works and its resale value, then make you an offer.

There's no obligation. If the offer works for you and the sale meets our purchasing requirements, we complete the purchase and pay you. If not, you take your device home.

Payment is in cash. There are no online quotes and no mail-in. Everything happens in person at the store. ID requirements and typical visit length to confirm
