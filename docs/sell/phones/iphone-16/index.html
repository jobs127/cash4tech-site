Bring your iPhone 16 to Cash4Tech in Gladstone for an in-person look and a cash offer. No listings, no strangers, no shipping.

About the iPhone 16

Released in 2024, the iPhone 16 family (16, 16 Plus, 16 Pro, 16 Pro Max) added the Camera Control button. Storage starts at 128 GB.

What we look at

Every device is evaluated in person. Things that can affect your offer include:

  • Model and generation
  • Storage capacity
  • Carrier and lock status
  • Battery health
  • Screen and body condition
  • Whether cameras, Face ID / fingerprint, buttons and charging work

We can't accept every device, and offers depend on condition and current demand. Not sure about yours? Call (816) 579-4440.

Before you come in

  • Back up to iCloud or a computer
  • Turn off Find My and sign out of your Apple ID. A device still locked to an Apple ID can't be resold
  • Know your passcode in case we need to check the device
  • Remove your SIM card

General guidance for getting a device ready. We can help in store if you get stuck.
