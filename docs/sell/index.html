What affects your offer

Every device is evaluated in person. Whether we can buy an item and what we offer depend on:

  • Model and age
  • Specs and storage
  • Physical condition
  • Whether everything works
  • Current resale demand
  • Accessories included

We can't accept every device, and offers aren't set in advance. If you're unsure about an item, give us a call at (816) 579-4440.
