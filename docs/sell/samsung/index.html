Sell your Samsung Phone

Galaxy S, Galaxy Z Fold or Flip, Note, or A series, bring your Samsung to Cash4Tech for an in-person look and an offer. Other Android phones are welcome to ask about too.

What we look at

Every samsung phone is evaluated in person. Things that can affect your offer include:

  • Model and generation
  • Storage capacity
  • Carrier and lock status
  • Screen condition, including burn-in
  • Body and back-glass condition
  • Whether cameras, charging and buttons all work

Not every device can be accepted, and offers depend on condition and current demand. Call ahead if you'd like to ask about yours.

Before you come in

A little prep makes the visit faster:

  • Back up your data to your Samsung or Google account
  • Remove your Google account and Samsung account from the phone
  • Know your PIN or pattern in case we need to check the device
  • Remove your SIM and any memory card

This is general guidance for getting a device ready. Ask us in store if you need a hand.
