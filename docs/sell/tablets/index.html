Sell your tablet

Tablets often get replaced long before they wear out. If yours is sitting unused, bring it to Cash4Tech for a cash offer.

By brandiPad

What we look at

Every device is evaluated in person. Things that can affect your offer include:

  • Model and generation
  • Storage capacity
  • Wi-Fi only or cellular
  • Screen and body condition
  • Battery health
  • Accessories such as a pencil or keyboard

We can't accept every device, and offers depend on condition and current demand. Not sure about yours? Call (816) 579-4440.

Before you come in

  • Back up anything you want to keep
  • Sign out of any accounts tied to the device
  • Bring chargers or accessories you'd like us to consider

General guidance for getting a device ready. We can help in store if you get stuck.

How it works

Three simple steps. No listings, no strangers.

Skip the marketplace hassle. Bring your device in, get an offer, and you decide.

Bring in your tech

Stop by our Gladstone store with the electronics you want to sell.

Get an offer

We look over the device's model, specs, condition and how well it works, then make you an offer.

Get cash

Like the offer? Accept it and we pay you in cash. Not for you? You're free to decline.

Quick answers

How will I be paid?

In cash, at the store, once you accept an offer.

Yes. You're always free to decline and take your device home.

Not at this time. Offers are made in person at the store after we've looked at the device.
