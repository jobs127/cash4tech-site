About the Nintendo Switch OLED

Released in 2021 with a 7-inch OLED screen, 64 GB of storage and a dock with a wired LAN port. Screen condition matters most.

What we look at

Every device is evaluated in person. Things that can affect your offer include:

  • Console model and storage
  • Whether it powers on and runs normally
  • Controllers and cables included
  • Disc drive or dock, where applicable
  • Cosmetic condition

We can't accept every device, and offers depend on condition and current demand. Not sure about yours? Call (816) 579-4440.

Before you come in

  • Back up your saves with Nintendo Switch Online or transfer them
  • Deregister it as your primary console in the eShop
  • Do a full system reset
  • Bring the dock, Joy-Con and power adapter

General guidance for getting a device ready. We can help in store if you get stuck.
