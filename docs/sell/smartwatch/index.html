Sell your Smartwatch

Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch and other wearables can be worth bringing in. Stop by Cash4Tech and we'll take a look.

What we look at

Every smartwatch is evaluated in person. Things that can affect your offer include:

  • Model, size and series
  • GPS only or cellular
  • Case material
  • Screen and case condition
  • Battery health
  • Whether the charger is included

Not every device can be accepted, and offers depend on condition and current demand. Call ahead if you'd like to ask about yours.

Before you come in

A little prep makes the visit faster:

  • Unpair the watch from your phone. This also removes Activation Lock on Apple Watch
  • Back up any health data you want to keep
  • Bring the charger if you have it

This is general guidance for getting a device ready. Ask us in store if you need a hand.
